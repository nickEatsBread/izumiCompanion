# izumi Companion v0.2.39

Adds TV-assisted source lookup when Cloudflare cannot reach Torrentio and the izumi desktop or mobile client is closed.

- The TV fetches public source metadata over your home connection, then the private Worker resolves playback through your configured debrid provider, including TorBox.
- Debrid credentials stay on the private Worker. Failed or cancelled lookups cannot resume playback after disconnecting or changing profiles.
- Retains the standalone catalogue recovery and retry fixes from 0.2.38.

The fallback requires a private Worker with TV-assisted source lookup support. Existing playback remains compatible with older Workers. Updating preserves the TV application's identity and saved pairing.

Includes Companion and Updater TV packages, Windows/macOS/Linux installers, Android APK, unsigned iOS app, and SHA256 checksums, all at version 0.2.39.

Verified source resolution with the updated private Worker and TorBox for Shrek and The Secret of Skinwalker Ranch using a home-network test harness. Physical-TV playback of this release has not yet been verified.
