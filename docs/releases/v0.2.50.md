# izumi Companion 0.2.50

- The private Worker now resolves the same primary source the desktop picks: a genuine digital release is no longer hidden behind camera copies that linger in the indexes after a film's digital release.
- Source ranking honours the same subtitle-language preference as the desktop.
- Releases whose only declared audio the TV cannot decode in-app (DTS, TrueHD, and Opus/FLAC on older TV engines) are skipped instead of preparing and playing silently. The TV now reports Opus/FLAC support to the Worker alongside AV1.
- Desktop and mobile installers bundle the matching Worker release.
